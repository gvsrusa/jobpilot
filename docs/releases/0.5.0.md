# JobPilot AI 0.5.0

Job discovery and job intelligence. Status: not published; builds are unsigned. This is a personal, non-commercial app. The owner waived AI subscription-policy and provider-terms concerns. That waiver does not reach any job source. The security controls (untrusted job text, no tools, consent records, validated output, no invented qualifications, no inferred work authorization) were not waived.

Placeholders named `PLACEHOLDER_...` are filled by the controller from a real run or a CI run. Nothing in this note was written from a run that did not happen, and a placeholder that is still there means the run did not happen yet.

## What was built

- **Connector SDK** (FR-13.5). A connector is a declaration (operations, automation, rate limit, policy status) and two pure functions, `request` and `parse`. It opens no socket. `check` refuses a declaration that is incomplete, and `gate` passes a read only when the build-time policy register says `allowed` or `silent-read` and the declaration agrees. See [Connector SDK](../connector-sdk.md).
- **One fetcher.** Every request goes through `connectors/fetch.rs`: the emergency pause, the gate, the declared host, a rate limit kept in the database (so the GUI, the CLI, and a restart share it), then the request. One honest `User-Agent`, no cookie, no proxy, no redirect. A challenge page, a 401, 403, 407, 451, or a redirect stops the source for 24 hours and tries nothing else. `Retry-After` is capped at 24 hours. A body over 16 MiB stores nothing.
- **Five read sources.** Lever (2 s, 300 a day), RemoteOK (15 minutes, 24 a day, with attribution), We Work Remotely (60 minutes, 24 a day, with attribution), Greenhouse and Ashby (2 s, 300 a day, on a board you name). Lever, RemoteOK, and We Work Remotely read on `allowed` rows. **Greenhouse and Ashby read on `silent-read` rows under ruling R1. R1 was a controller ruling made while planning this release. It is not an owner decision.** Both vendors' documents address the employer and say nothing about a third-party reader. The owner has not been asked, and can overturn it. Setting the row and the declaration to `unknown` turns a read off.
- **Four assisted sources.** Workday, Indeed, LinkedIn, and a company's own career page are never requested. You paste the posting, or its text with its link. A pasted Lever, Greenhouse, or Ashby link alone is not fetched: the answer names `job source add <connector> <board>`.
- **A job store** (migrations 0013 to 0015, schema version 15). Sources, jobs, companies, versioned job texts, duplicate groups, match results, and company notes. Existing pasted job texts become jobs on upgrade. A duplicate needs a matching place, and a person's split survives the next fetch. A job with no company is tracked as "Company not stated".
- **Rules that read a posting.** Place and country (a two-letter suffix that is both a region and a country code stays unknown, and so does a place in several countries), pay (a model never sets pay; with no stated period a number is a year only at 20,000 or more), seniority, work mode, and skills. English first.
- **A score you can read.** Skills 40, seniority 20, location 20, pay 20. A criterion that cannot be compared is left out, unknown is never a mismatch, and the screen shows the compared weight. Objective filters keep a job that does not state the field and say so.
- **A model's evaluation, kept apart.** `semantic_match` and `summarize_company` are two new router tasks. The evaluation shows fit, summary, strengths that cite real evidence, and gaps, and never changes the score, a filter, or the order. `parse_job` may fill blanks the rules left empty: a model sets work mode or seniority only when the word is in the posting and is not negated, and on a revised posting only your own fields stay.
- **Three agents** on the workflow runner: discovery (fetch, then dedupe, no model), matching (fields, score, semantic), and research (gather, summarize). Research reads stored text only and holds no connector permission. Matching holds no `read:sensitive`.
- **Work authorization** shows as exactly one line, by exact country, and is never scored, filtered on, or stated as eligible. A posting that mentions it gets a second line.
- **Import** refuses an http link and a link with a user name or a port. Rust opens a stored link, from the job's id, over https only.
- **CLI and screen.** `jobpilot job paste|import|list|search|show|remove|split|sources|source|fetch|analyze|research|company|prefs`, and the Jobs view on Ctrl/Cmd+0 with a job detail that explains its score. The `job show` JSON shape is in [architecture](../architecture.md#62-cratescli-jobpilot).
- **Injection corpus** files 20 (hidden HTML from a connector), 21 (fit-score steering), and 22 (company claims), run through the three tasks.
- Migrations 0013, 0014, and 0015. Schema version 15. A 0.4 database (version 12) upgrades after a `pre-migrate-12-*` snapshot. Version 0.5.0.

## Known issues

- No cap on postings per source beyond the 16 MiB body cap and Lever's five pages (500 postings). The 0.5 plan named a 500-posting cap for every source; it was not built.
- A model's wording is checked by word lists and grounding rules, and a paraphrase can pass. This was the last grounding round for `parse_job`, `semantic_match`, and `summarize_company`; residuals go to the final review.
- Surviving mutants parked by the Task 9 and 10 reviews: a cancel can be swallowed by the runner, and some stop points after a model reply are not tested.
- Parked from Task 8: an on-site job with a stated location of "Anywhere" scores 20 on location, and the location note shows a raw country code.
- Parked from Task 11: the `--all` flag, the `Locked` authorization text, and a harmless pause check have no test.
- `unusable_reply` ends "write this one yourself", which fits a draft better than an evaluation. The text is shared.
- The 0.5 plan's Task 15 expects `schema 13`. The schema is 15.
- The Jobs screen can add a source but never remove one, and after 50 typed boards Fetch stops working until `jobpilot job source remove` clears some. A We Work Remotely category feed and a `--query` filter exist only in the CLI.
- "Acme Inc" and "Acme" are one company for grouping duplicates, but two for research, notes, and `job company list`.
- A person pasting text gets no location parse: a place named in the text is not read, so location reads as not compared.
- `Go` in Go-to-market reads as Golang, and a company named as a customer can read as a skill.

## Outstanding risks and parked items

Rulings made during the build, each a deliberate cost:

- **R1 is a controller ruling, not an owner decision.** Greenhouse and Ashby are read while their vendors are silent. If a vendor objects, a request can be blocked; no account is involved. Cost if wrong: flip two rows and two declarations.
- **The name "D3".** `docs/policy-register.md` and the PRD use D3 for unsigned 0.x installers. The 0.5 build shares the name in some text, a test name, and one user-visible note. It was not renamed.
- **No owner record of R1.** The ledger found none in the register, the PRD, or project memory, and the owner's waiver covers vendor terms for AI providers only.
- **Duplicates need matching place.** An unknown country never matches on title alone. Cost: some true duplicates stay visible.
- **Link comparison keeps query parameters** except tracking ones, so two jobs that differ in `gh_jid` do not merge. Cost: some true duplicates stay visible.
- **Unknown is never a mismatch.** An unknown place, mode, or pay is left out of the score, not scored low. Cost: unknown jobs rank mid-list.
- **No stated period means a year only at 20,000 or more.** Cost: real yearly pay in low-denominated figures is not compared.
- **A model never sets pay.** Cost: a posting whose pay the rules miss shows none. RemoteOK's pay period stays unknown.
- **A model sets work mode or seniority only when its word is in the posting.** Cost: fewer model fills.
- **On a revised posting only your fields stay.** Model fields reset because they came from the old text. Cost: one more model call per changed posting.
- **Import refuses http links and links with a user name or port.** Cost: edit the link to https.
- **A two-letter place suffix that is both a region and a country code stays unknown**, unless a city in a 189-city table settles it. Cost: fewer jobs filter by country, none wrongly.
- **`Retry-After` is capped at 24 hours**, matching the longest hold. Cost: one extra request a day to a server that asks for more.
- **A refusal keeps a source quiet for a day,** and a person cannot override it. Cost: a one-off 403 holds a source for 24 hours.
- **A job with no company tracks as "Company not stated".** Cost: a label to edit.
- **Greenhouse links need https only,** since `absolute_url` can name the employer's domain. Cost: an employer link can open off the vendor's host.
- **Old text versions stay on disk** until the job is removed. Data delete still clears them.
- **A posting's own link can point anywhere,** as any link can. The button names the host first.
- A fake posting from a feed can still score well. See the [threat model](../security/threat-model.md#11-residual-risks).

## Upgrading

Open a 0.4 database with 0.5 and the app writes `backups/pre-migrate-12-<stamp>/` first, then applies migrations 0013 to 0015. Restoring that folder returns the 0.4 data. There is no down migration.

Every job text you pasted before becomes a job with the source `Imported`, the rules read it once when the database first opens, and your drafts keep pointing at their text. No AI consent changes: the policy register's AI provider rows were not edited, so no consent record is suspended. A new task may need a category you have not given. Research needs `company_text` as well as `job_text`, and the evaluation needs `resume_text` as well. Without it the router skips that provider and the task ends `manual_required` with a consent reason such as `consent_needed`; the rules score stands.

Nothing fetches until you press Fetch or run `jobpilot job fetch`. A source with a stored hold, from a refusal or a `Retry-After`, shows when it may be read again.
